Health visitor guide

What to bring to a health visitor appointment.

A short checklist for a health visitor visit or baby review, and how to show your baby's feeding, nappy and sleep history from the Tiny Logger baby tracker app.

Your child's Red Book

In the UK, bring your child's personal child health record, often called the Red Book. Your health visitor uses it to record weight, height and reviews.

A picture of feeding

How often your baby feeds, which side or how much from a bottle, and any pumping. A rough pattern over the last few days is more useful than one day.

Wet and dirty nappies

How many wet and dirty nappies a day, and anything unusual about colour or consistency that you want to ask about.

Sleep

Roughly how long and how often your baby sleeps, and anything about sleep that worries you.

Medicines, allergies and vaccinations

Any medicines your child takes, known allergies, and which vaccinations they have had.

Your questions

Write questions down as they come up during the week, so you don't forget them in the appointment.

Using Tiny Logger: show it from your phone

Show your logs

Open History in Tiny Logger to show feeds, nappies and sleep by day, with daily totals.

Export a summary

Create a PDF health summary with allergies, medicines and vaccinations, and add sleep and growth if you want. You can show it on your phone or share it.

Bring your questions

Add the appointment in Tiny Logger and note questions as they come, so they're ready when you arrive.

This is not medical advice

Tiny Logger helps you keep records. It does not diagnose. For what happens at each review, see the NHS page on baby reviews. If your baby is unwell or you are worried, contact your GP, health visitor, NHS 111 or emergency services.
