Health visitor guide

What to bring to a health visitor appointment.

A short checklist for a health visitor visit or baby review, and how to show your baby's feeding, nappy and sleep history from the Tiny Logger baby tracker app.

Before you go

  • Your child's Red BookIn the UK, bring your child's personal child health record. Your health visitor uses it to record weight, height and reviews.
  • A picture of feedingHow often your baby feeds, which side or how much from a bottle, and any pumping. A pattern over the last few days is more useful than one day.
  • Wet and dirty nappiesHow many a day, and anything about colour or consistency you want to ask about.
  • SleepRoughly how long and how often your baby sleeps, and anything about sleep that worries you.
  • Medicines, allergies and vaccinationsAny medicines your child takes, known allergies, and which vaccinations they've had.
  • Your questionsWrite them down as they come up during the week, so you don't forget them on the day.

Show it from Tiny Logger

  1. Note questions as they comeAdd the appointment in Tiny Logger and write questions under it during the week.
  2. Show your historyOn the day, open History to show feeds, nappies and sleep by day, with daily totals.
  3. Share a summaryCreate a PDF health summary with allergies, medicines and vaccinations, and add sleep and growth if you want.

This is not medical advice

Tiny Logger helps you keep records. It does not diagnose. For what happens at each review, see the NHS page on baby reviews. If your baby is unwell or you are worried, contact your GP, health visitor, NHS 111 or emergency services.
