FAQ

Questions parents ask about Tiny Logger.

Tiny Logger is a free baby tracker and family care app for iPhone and iPad. Here are plain answers to what people ask most.

Download on the App Store

What is Tiny Logger?

Tiny Logger is a free baby tracker and family care app for iPhone and iPad. You can log feeds, breastfeeding sides, bottles, pumping, sleep, awake windows, nappies, solids, vitamins, tummy time, weight and height. It also keeps medicines, allergies, vaccinations and appointments for each child in one place.

Is Tiny Logger free?

Yes. Tiny Logger is free to download on the App Store, with no subscription and no in-app purchases. It has no adverts. Market includes optional product links, and some of them may earn Tiny Logger a commission at no extra cost to you.

Does Tiny Logger work on iPhone, iPad and Mac?

Tiny Logger works on iPhone and iPad running iOS 17 or later. It is not available for Mac or Android.

Can my partner and I share Tiny Logger?

Yes. Invite a partner, grandparents or a nanny through private iCloud sharing. Everyone logs to the same child, timers stay in sync, and you choose what each person can see and whether they can edit. You can remove access at any time.

Where is my baby's data stored?

Your child's records stay on your devices and in your own iCloud. You choose who sees them, and Tiny Logger never sells your data. Apple Health access is optional and read-only.

Can I track more than one child separately?

Yes. Each child gets their own profile, so twins and siblings never have their logs or records mixed. You can set each profile to baby, toddler or child, and Tiny Logger shows what fits that stage.

Can I export records for a GP or health visitor?

Yes. Tiny Logger can create a PDF health summary for a child, with allergies, medicines and vaccinations, and you can add sleep and growth. You can also note questions before an appointment and take them with you.

Can I move my history from another baby tracker?

Yes. You can import your history from Huckleberry and Nara Baby, so the feeds and naps you have already logged come with you.

Is Tiny Logger a medical app?

No. Tiny Logger helps you organise everyday care and records. It does not diagnose and does not replace medical advice. If you are worried about your child, contact your GP, midwife, health visitor or emergency services.

Is Tiny Logger available in Japanese?

Not yet. Tiny Logger is currently in English. It includes UK and US guidance, such as NHS sleep guidance and WHO or CDC growth charts, chosen by your region.

Is Tiny Logger the same as Tinylog or Tiny Tracker?

No. Tiny Logger is a separate app, made by Motonari Shiga under the Okkle Lab name, and is not affiliated with Tinylog or Tiny Tracker. Its App Store listing is "Tiny Logger: Baby Tracker".
