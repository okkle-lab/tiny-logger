Which one suits you

Huckleberry

For expert-led sleep guidance, custom sleep plans or an AI parenting chat, if you're happy to pay for a membership. It's the most established of the three for sleep.

Baby Tracker by Nighp

For an Apple Watch app, a long track record, or the app in Japanese or another language. Core tracking is free, with an optional subscription for extras.

Tiny Logger

For everything free with no subscription, and one place for each child's medicines, allergies, vaccinations and appointments as well as daily logs. It's newer, with far fewer ratings so far.
