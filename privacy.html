Privacy

Your family’s information is personal.

Tiny Logger is designed to help families organise everyday care. We aim to make clear what information is used, when it is shared, and how optional retailer links work.

Information you choose to add

Tiny Logger may store the information you choose to enter or attach, such as care logs, notes, appointments, health records, supply lists and photos. Please only add information you are comfortable keeping in the app.

Sharing and sync

Where app features offer family sharing or syncing, you choose whether to use them and who can access shared information. You can stop using optional sharing features and delete information you no longer want to keep.

Product recommendations and affiliate links

Essentials may show optional product recommendations or links to retailers. Some links are affiliate links, which means Tiny Logger may receive a commission if you choose to purchase through them, at no additional cost to you.

Retailers may receive standard link-tracking information needed to attribute a purchase, such as the retailer page visited and a link identifier. Their own privacy policies apply once you leave Tiny Logger.

How recommendations are selected

Recommendations may be based on broad child age stages, the type of essentials you choose to track, and choices you make within Essentials, such as hiding or saving an item. They are optional and you can always buy elsewhere.

We do not use private journal entries, photos, conversations, symptoms, medical records, or HealthKit data to select commercial recommendations. Recommendations are not medical advice.

Your choices

  • You can ignore, hide or dismiss recommendations.
  • You can choose not to use shopping links.
  • You can buy from any retailer you prefer.
  • You can contact us with privacy questions or requests.

Questions about privacy?

Email admin@okklelab.com. We will keep this page updated as Tiny Logger develops and before public release.
