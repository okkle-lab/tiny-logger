Privacy policy

Your family’s data is personal.

We designed Tiny Logger to help parents and caregivers organise everyday care, not to turn a child’s information into a product.

Last updated 2 September 2026

Private by design

Your child’s records are for your family and the people you choose to share them with.

Optional, always

Photos, camera, notifications, location, Apple Health, sharing and sync are only used when you choose them.

Who is responsible?

Tiny Logger is operated by Motonari Shiga, trading as Okkle Lab. For privacy questions, contact admin@okklelab.com.

Information you choose to add

  • Child profiles, care logs, growth records and milestones.
  • Health-related records such as medications, allergies, vaccinations, visits, symptoms and care notes.
  • Journal entries, photos, attachments and Ask Okkle messages.
  • Details needed for optional reminders, weather, Apple Health or Apple Watch features.

How Tiny Logger uses information

  • To display, organise and, where you enable it, sync your family’s records.
  • To provide reminders, trends, summaries, document scanning and app actions.
  • To let you export records or share them with caregivers you choose.
  • To keep Tiny Logger reliable, secure and safe to use.

Permissions and optional services

Tiny Logger asks for a permission only for a feature you choose: Photos and Camera for journal images or document scanning; Notifications for reminders; Location for local weather; and Apple Health or Apple Watch only for the categories you authorise. You can change permissions in iPhone Settings.

Sharing, syncing and export

You choose whether to share records with a caregiver, use iCloud syncing or export selected data. Share exported health or child information only with people you trust.

Ask Okkle and external sources

Ask Okkle provides general, source-based information and app assistance. It is not a doctor, does not diagnose or prescribe, and does not replace professional medical advice. For urgent concerns, contact an appropriate healthcare professional or emergency service.

Retention, email and your choices

Records remain in Tiny Logger until you delete them. You can review, edit, export or delete records in the app. If you email us for support or launch updates, we use the information in that email to respond or notify you. Please do not include sensitive child health information by email.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Tiny Logger or legal requirements change. The current version will always be published here.
