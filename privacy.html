Privacy

Privacy, made clearer.

A simple explanation of what you choose to add, share and use—without the legal fog.

Last updated: September 2026

What you add

Tiny Logger stores the care logs, notes, appointments, health records, supply lists and photos you choose to enter or attach.

Sharing and sync

These are optional. You decide whether to use them, who can access shared information, and what you no longer want to keep.

Retailer links

Essentials may include optional retailer links. Some may earn Tiny Logger a commission at no extra cost to you. A retailer’s own privacy policy applies after you leave Tiny Logger.

How recommendations work

They may use broad child age stages, tracked essential types and your Essentials choices—not photos, conversations, symptoms, medical records or HealthKit data.

Questions about privacy?

We’re listening.

Email admin@okklelab.com. Please do not send sensitive child health information by email.
