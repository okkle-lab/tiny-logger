Need urgent medical help?

Tiny Logger is not a medical service and does not replace a doctor. If a child is seriously unwell or you are worried, contact local urgent medical care or emergency services immediately.

Getting started

Set up a child profile, choose the logs you use, then make Today work around your family’s real routine.

Logging and timers

Record feeds, sleep, nappies, pumping, food, vitamins, weight, height and other care events for your child’s stage.

Health and records

Keep medications, allergies, vaccinations, appointments and care notes together. Review scanned information before you save it.

Sharing and privacy

Each child has separate records. You choose optional sharing, iCloud syncing and data export.

Contact
