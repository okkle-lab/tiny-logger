Support

Help for every little moment.

Tiny Logger helps families record care, health information, growth, sleep, milestones, and memories in one private place.

Email support

Need urgent medical help?

Tiny Logger is not a medical service and does not replace a doctor. If a child is seriously unwell or you are worried, contact local urgent medical care or emergency services immediately.

Quick Logs

Record feeds, nappies, pumping, food, vitamins, sleep, weight, height, and other care events relevant to your child’s stage.

Health records

Keep medications, allergies, vaccinations, appointments, and care notes together. Review all scanned document details before saving.

Sleep and trends

Start and stop sleep sessions, then view patterns alongside growth and other care trends over time.

Journal and milestones

Save photos, notes, and special milestones while keeping routine care records in History.

Ask Okkle

Ask Okkle can help find information in your saved care records, assist with app actions, and provide general source-based health information. It is not a doctor, does not diagnose or prescribe, and should not be relied on for urgent medical decisions.

Privacy, sharing, and export

Each child has separate records. You control optional features such as photos, camera, notifications, location, Apple Health, Apple Watch, caregiver sharing, and data export. When exporting, you can choose the child and categories to include.

Troubleshooting

My data is not appearing

Confirm that the correct child profile is selected, then restart the app. If you use iCloud sync, check that it is enabled on the device.

A document scan is incorrect

Use a clear, well-lit image with all text visible. Always review and amend the recognised details before confirming.
