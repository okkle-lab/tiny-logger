A few quick fixes

Troubleshooting

01

My data is not appearing

Check that the correct child profile is selected, then restart the app. If you use iCloud, confirm syncing is enabled on that device.

02

A document scan is incorrect

Use a clear, well-lit image with all text visible. Review and amend the extracted details before you confirm them.

03

Reminders are not arriving

Check iPhone Settings → Notifications → Tiny Logger, then confirm the relevant reminder is on in the app.
