Tiny Logger Support

Help for tracking your child’s care, health records, growth, sleep, milestones, and family memories.

Need urgent medical help?
Tiny Logger is not a medical service and does not replace a doctor. If a child is seriously unwell, has breathing difficulty, is unresponsive, has blue/grey skin or lips, or you are worried, contact emergency services or local urgent medical care immediately.

Getting started

Create a child profile, choose the relevant care stage, and select the Quick Logs you want to use. Each child has separate logs, health records, settings, and optional Apple Health / Apple Watch assignment.

Using Tiny Logger

Today and Quick Log

Use Quick Log to record feeds, nappies, pumping, food, vitamins, sleep, weight, height, and other care events available for your child’s stage. The Today screen highlights current care information and upcoming patterns only when enough previous entries exist.

Sleep tracking

Start a sleep session when your child goes to sleep, then stop it when they wake. Tiny Logger saves the duration and can show sleep trends over time.

Health records

Store allergies, medications, vaccinations, appointments, and care notes. You can use the camera or photo library to capture documents, then review and confirm extracted details before anything is saved.

Growth and milestones

Record height and weight to see growth trends. Milestones can be logged as memories and celebrated in the app. Growth charts are for context only and are not medical advice.

Journal

Save notes, photos, and milestones to remember important moments. Care logs remain in History.

Ask Okkle

Ask Okkle can help you find saved care information, assist with app actions, and provide general, source-based health information. It is not a doctor, does not diagnose, prescribe, or replace professional medical advice. Always seek professional help for urgent concerns.

Your privacy
Your child’s information is private. You choose whether to use optional features such as Photos, Camera, Notifications, Location, Apple Health, Apple Watch, sharing, or data export.

Data, sharing, and export

You can export selected child data and choose which categories to include. When sharing with a caregiver, choose exactly which child’s data is shared. Do not export or share data unless you trust the recipient.

Apple Health and Apple Watch

Apple Health and Apple Watch connections are optional and assigned per child profile. You control the categories Tiny Logger can read or write in Apple Health settings.

Troubleshooting

My data is not appearing

Check that you have selected the correct child profile and that sync is enabled if you use iCloud. Restarting the app may refresh the display.

A photo or document did not scan correctly

Use a clear, well-lit photo with all text visible. Review and amend the extracted information before confirming it.

Notifications are not arriving

Check iPhone Settings → Notifications → Tiny Logger, and confirm that the relevant reminder is turned on in the app.

Contact support

Email admin@okklelab.com.

Please include your device model, iOS version, and a description of the issue. Do not send sensitive child health information by email.
